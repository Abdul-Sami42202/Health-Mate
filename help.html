Help & FAQs

Everything you need to know about using HealthMate.

dashboard Dashboard expand_more

Your Dashboard shows your most recent vitals, your latest uploaded reports, and an AI-generated health tip based on your recent entries.

Use the Manual Entry form to log a Blood Pressure, Blood Sugar, or Weight reading. Once you've logged a few, your vitals cards will show a trend chart, and an AI tip will appear once a day based on your latest readings.

description Uploading Reports expand_more

Go to Reports to upload a lab report or scan (PDF, JPG, or PNG). Fill in the title, date, and category, then choose whether to enable AI Summary.

With AI Summary enabled, HealthMate analyzes your report and generates a plain-language summary, flags any values outside the normal range, suggests questions for your doctor, and gives basic dietary guidance. With it disabled, only the report itself is saved and shown.

Every report you've processed appears on your Timeline, and can be viewed, downloaded as a PDF, or shared at any time.

timeline Timeline expand_more

The Timeline brings together your uploaded reports, logged vitals, and AI-generated insights in one chronological view, grouped by month.

Use the filter chips at the top to show only Reports, only Vitals, or only AI Insights. Every entry can be deleted directly from its card, except for AI insights, which regenerate automatically.

family_restroom Family Profiles expand_more

Add family members from the Family page to keep their contact details, blood group, and emergency contact information in one place.

Health records (reports, vitals, and AI insights) are tracked only for your own account, so family member profiles show contact and identification details rather than health data.

Your own profile card is marked Primary and can't be removed, since it's linked to your account.

person Your Profile expand_more

Your Profile page shows your personal details, emergency contact, and preferences. Tap Edit Profile to update any of this information, including your profile picture.

privacy_tip Privacy & Data expand_more

Your records are only accessible from your own account. See the full details in our Privacy Policy.
