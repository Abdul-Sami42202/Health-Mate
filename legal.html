Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we store

HealthMate stores the information you choose to enter: your profile details, family member profiles, uploaded medical reports, manually logged vitals, and AI-generated summaries of that data. This data is stored in your account and is only accessible to you.

How uploaded reports are processed

When AI Summary is enabled, an uploaded report is sent to a third-party AI service to generate a summary, flagged values, and suggested questions. This happens only when you choose to enable it for that upload.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your health data to third parties.
  • We don't share your records with anyone else, including other family members you've added, unless you take an action that explicitly does so.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models.

Your control over your data

You can edit or delete any report, vital entry, or family profile at any time from within the app. Deleting your account removes your stored records.
